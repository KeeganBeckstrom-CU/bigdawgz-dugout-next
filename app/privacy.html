Privacy Policy

Basepaths is a team app for youth baseball coaches, players and families. This policy explains what we keep, why, and the choices you have. Plain version: we keep only what your team needs to use the app, we never sell it, and we never show ads.

What we keep

  • Accounts: name, email (or a username for kids), password (stored scrambled, never readable), and which teams you're on.
  • Team info: roster, schedule, practice plans, drills assigned, and team settings a coach enters, including team logos and photos.
  • Activity: drills watched, games and quizzes played, practice check-offs and points, so coaches and parents can see progress.
  • Drill videos: short clips (up to 60 seconds) a player chooses to send their coach. They're stored privately, only that team's coaches can watch them, and each clip is deleted as soon as the coach marks it watched. Unfinished uploads are deleted after a day.
  • Payments: dues amounts a coach records. We don't take or store card or bank numbers; payments happen in apps like Venmo. Coaches record the amount, date and method of team dues and fundraising so each family can see what they've paid.

Kids under 13

Players under 13 can't sign themselves up. A parent creates the player's account (name, username, password) in My teams → My players and checks a box giving their permission. We record which parent gave permission, when, and the wording they agreed to. We collect only what the team uses: the player's name, username, app activity, and any drill videos the player sends their coach. Parents can see their player's info, change the password, or delete the player's account and everything on it at any time from My players. We don't share a child's information with anyone outside their team, and we don't use it for ads or anything unrelated to the team app.

Who can see it

People on your team see team info, and coaches see players' progress. A parent sees their own player's info, payments and fundraising, and nobody else's. The Basepaths admin can see account and team details to run the service and help with problems. We use Supabase to store data and GitHub Pages to host the app. They store it for us and may not use it for anything else.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When an account is deleted, it's removed right away, along with the player's activity. Backup copies are cleared within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time: My teams → Delete my account.
  • Parents: delete or change a player's account in My players.
  • Leave a team from your account page inside the team.
